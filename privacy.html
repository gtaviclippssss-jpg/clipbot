clipbot - Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What clipbot is

clipbot is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (and other social accounts the owner controls).

What access it requests

When the owner signs in with Google, clipbot requests two permissions: uploading videos to YouTube, and read-only access to the name of the channel being used (so the owner can confirm the right channel was chosen).

What data is collected and where it is stored

clipbot does not collect personal data from anyone. The sign-in token that Google issues is stored only in a file on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server operated by the developer, and no analytics or tracking are used.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties. clipbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke clipbot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
